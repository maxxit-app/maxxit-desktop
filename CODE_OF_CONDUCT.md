# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique the work and explain the
technical concern. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private
information, or repeatedly derail discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask for changes, close a thread,
or restrict participation. Decisions should explain the relevant behavior and
allow a private appeal. These rules apply to repository issues, PRs, and official
Maxxit community spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to contact@maxxit.app. Do not post identifying
details in public. The repository owner handles reports confidentially and
avoids involving people with a conflict of interest.
